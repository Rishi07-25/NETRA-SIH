# Innovation & Technical Novelty

## Differentiating Advantages of NETRA

| Conventional Solutions (NIDS / SIEM) | NETRA (Network Threat Early-warning & Risk Analytics) |
|---|---|
| **Reactive Detection:** Triggers after signature match or threshold breach. | **Temporal Forecasting:** Projects future risk trajectory across forward time windows ($t+\Delta t$). |
| **Isolated Alerts:** Evaluates packets or individual connections in silos. | **Sequential Context:** Correlates time-windowed flow progressions along attack kill chains. |
| **Binary Classification:** Alerts purely on "Normal" vs "Malicious". | **Hierarchical Ensemble:** Combines unsupervised anomaly scores, multi-class threat typing, and risk forecasting. |
| **High Alert Volume:** Floods security operators with unprioritized alarms. | **Synthesized Threat Score:** Single dynamic metric prioritizing high-imminence threat vectors. |
| **Post-Incident Response:** MTTR measured in hours/days after damage occurs. | **Pre-Emptive Early Warning:** Enables automated firewall shunning or rate-limiting *before* saturation. |

---

## Key Pillars of Innovation
1. **Time-Horizon Risk Projection:** Moving beyond instantaneous point classification to multi-step temporal risk extrapolation.
2. **Behavioral Precursor Correlation:** Linking subtle low-rate reconnaissance directly to forecasted volumetric or credential-stuffing follow-ups.
3. **Analyst-Centric Visualization:** Transforming complex statistical probability curves into mission-critical SOC tactical insights.
