# Research & Literature References

## Key Research Areas
1. **Time-Series Cyber Threat Forecasting:** Using temporal patterns in network telemetry to anticipate attack escalation.
2. **Network Flow Behavioral Analysis:** Differentiating benign high-load bursts from distributed reconnaissance and denial of service.
3. **Multi-Stage Cyber Attack Modeling:** Mapping statistical precursors across the MITRE ATT&CK lifecycle.
